## 📋 How Products Display

### Homepage (index.html):
- **Does NOT show individual products**
- Shows **6 category cards** that link to category pages
- This is intentional design

### Category Pages (category-*.html):
- **Shows filtered products** for that category
- Example: category-bumpers.html shows only bumper products
- Each product has:
  - ❤️ Wishlist/Favorites button
  - Product image
  - Product name & price
  - **VIEW DETAILS** button (links to product page)

### Product Pages (product-*.html):
- Individual product detail pages
- Shows full product information
- **ADD TO CART** button (only on product pages)

---

## 🔍 Where to See Products

1. **Homepage** → Click any category card (e.g., "Front Bumpers")
2. **Category Page** → See all products in that category
3. **Click "VIEW DETAILS"** → Go to individual product page
