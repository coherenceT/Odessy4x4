HOW WE BUILD IT

Every Odyssey product goes through a rigorous 5-step engineering process.

Step 01

3D Design & CAD

Every product starts as a precision 3D CAD model, engineered for exact vehicle fitment.

Step 02

CNC Laser Cutting

High-grade steel is laser cut to micron accuracy, ensuring perfect edges every time.

Step 03

Expert Welding

Hand-MIG and TIG welded by our experienced team for maximum structural integrity.

Step 04

Powder Coating

Heavy-duty premium powder coat applied for superior corrosion and chip resistance.

Step 05

QA & Dispatch

Rigorous quality inspection and fitment checks before your gear leaves our workshop.
