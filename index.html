OUR HISTORY

Odyssey 4x4 was born from a passion for exploring the unforgiving African landscape. For years, we have been designing and manufacturing premium 4x4 accessories that withstand the toughest conditions.

Our commitment is to quality, durability, and a rugged aesthetic that transforms your vehicle into the ultimate expedition machine. Every single component leaves our workshop having passed our rigorous quality standard checks.

HOW WE BUILD IT

Every Odyssey product goes through a rigorous 5-step engineering process.

01 ⚡

3D Design & CAD

Every product starts as a precision 3D CAD model, engineered for exact vehicle fitment.

02 🔆

CNC Laser Cutting

High-grade steel is laser cut to micron accuracy, ensuring perfect edges every time.

03 🔥

Expert Welding

Hand-MIG and TIG welded by our experienced team for maximum structural integrity.

04 🎨

Powder Coating

Heavy-duty premium powder coat applied for superior corrosion and chip resistance.

05 🏁

QA & Dispatch

Rigorous quality inspection and fitment checks before your gear leaves our workshop.
