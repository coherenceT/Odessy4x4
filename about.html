ABOUT ODYSSEY 4X4

Engineered for Adventure Since 1999

| OUR STORY

GOBI-X HAS BEEN A MAJOR 4WD ACCESSORIES MANUFACTURER AND DISTRIBUTOR SINCE 1999.

Gobi-X's 4WD products are renowned, loved and operated by 4WD enthusiasts globally. Gobi-X offers a comprehensive range of accessories and equipment to suit almost every 4x4 vehicles. Our wide product range offers customers the choice of accessories such as Front Bull Bar, Rear Bar, Rear Carrier Bar, Side Steps, Roof Rack, Drawer system etc.

Our experience and technical expertise in manufacturing 4WD accessories enable us to supply our customers with a wide variety of products which will improve the vehicle's appearance, performance and functionality.
